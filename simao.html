01. ABOUT ME

Curiosity for everything with a screen and circuits

I have always loved exploring the world of technology. I enjoy understanding how devices work inside and out, testing different operating systems, and preserving phones and tablets from various eras.

Right now, I am focused on learning HTML and CSS. This very website is part of my learning journey — a personal space where I can experiment with code, organize my ideas, and showcase my tech collection.

I am also a huge fan of Linux, open-source philosophy, and Linus Torvalds — the genius behind the Linux kernel (and Git!) that powers so much of the tech world I love, including Android!
